Why does this matter?
Landfills are a major source of methane, a powerful climate pollutant, and can also release other pollutants that affect nearby communities. Yet information about landfill pollution can be difficult to find, and in most states, landfill operators are not required to investigate or respond when large methane plumes are detected by satellites or aircraft.
That's beginning to change. Starting in 2027, California and Colorado will require landfill operators to investigate certain large remotely detected methane emissions. These policies recognize a simple idea: finding emissions is the first step toward fixing them.
By staying informed, you can help bring greater transparency and accountability to landfill pollution, support stronger solutions to reduce emissions, and protect nearby communities.
